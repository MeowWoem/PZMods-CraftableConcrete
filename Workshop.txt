description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=[i]When the world crumbles, so do your walls. Wood rots, metal rusts, and zombies really don't respect property lines. But long before drywall and duct tape, humanity already knew the answer to "how do I stop this from falling apart": mix rock, sand and a burning desire to survive.
description=So grab your bucket, channel your inner Roman engineer, and start pouring your way to a base that doesn't collapse the moment a horde leans on it.[/i]
description=
description=This mod allows you to craft concrete powder using quicklime, sand and gravel, giving you a proper use for that kiln you built and never knew what to do with.
description=
description=The recipe is loosely based on real lime concrete, the same kind of mix the Romans used long before Portland cement existed. So yes, technically, your safehouse can now be built on the same principles as the Colosseum.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting Process[/h2]
description=Making concrete is refreshingly straightforward, as long as you've already got your Quicklime production going.
description=
description=[b]1. Mix the Concrete Powder[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Quicklime
description=[*] 2 Bag of Sand
description=[*] 3 Bag of Gravel
description=[*] 1 Bucket (kept)
description=[/list]
